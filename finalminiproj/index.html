Paoay

The Municipality of Timeless Heritage

Paoay is known as “The Municipality of Timeless Heritage” because its culture, architecture, and traditions have stood the test of time. From the centuries-old Paoay Church, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, to its well-preserved ancestral houses and deep-rooted Ilocano customs, Paoay embodies a living heritage that continues to thrive through generations.
